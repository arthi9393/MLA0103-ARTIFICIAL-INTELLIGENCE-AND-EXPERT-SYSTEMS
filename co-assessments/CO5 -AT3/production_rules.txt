AUTOMOBILE FAULT DIAGNOSIS – PRODUCTION RULE MODEL

R1:
IF engine_overheating AND coolant_low
THEN cooling_system_failure.

R2:
IF warning_light_on AND engine_overheating
THEN engine_malfunction.

R3:
IF starting_failure AND battery_weak
THEN battery_problem.

R4:
IF abnormal_noise AND engine_oil_low
THEN lubrication_problem.

R5:
IF low_mileage AND fuel_consumption_high
THEN fuel_system_problem.

Example:
Facts:
engine_overheating
coolant_low

Using R1:
engine_overheating AND coolant_low
        ↓
cooling_system_failure
