AUTOMOBILE FAULT DIAGNOSIS – FIRST-ORDER LOGIC MODEL

PREDICATES

Overheating(x)
LowCoolant(x)
WarningLight(x)
StartingFailure(x)
WeakBattery(x)
AbnormalNoise(x)
LowOil(x)
LowMileage(x)
HighFuelConsumption(x)

CoolingSystemFailure(x)
EngineMalfunction(x)
BatteryProblem(x)
LubricationProblem(x)
FuelSystemProblem(x)

FOL RULES

R1:
∀x [(Overheating(x) ∧ LowCoolant(x)) → CoolingSystemFailure(x)]

R2:
∀x [(WarningLight(x) ∧ Overheating(x)) → EngineMalfunction(x)]

R3:
∀x [(StartingFailure(x) ∧ WeakBattery(x)) → BatteryProblem(x)]

R4:
∀x [(AbnormalNoise(x) ∧ LowOil(x)) → LubricationProblem(x)]

R5:
∀x [(LowMileage(x) ∧ HighFuelConsumption(x)) → FuelSystemProblem(x)]

FACTS

LowCoolant(car1)
WarningLight(car1)

EXAMPLE

Overheating(car1) and LowCoolant(car1) satisfy R1.
Therefore:
CoolingSystemFailure(car1)
